Product safety

OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100

Our white and dyed 100% polyester sewing thread is certified under OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100, an independent testing and certification system for textiles tested for harmful substances. The certificate was issued by AITEX, an OEKO-TEX® member institute in Alcoy, Spain, and covers Product Class I (baby articles), the class with the strictest requirements.
